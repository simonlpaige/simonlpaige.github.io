Our story

Three generations on West 85th.

Angelo started the greenhouse in 1972 with a single structure and a plot of land on West 85th Street. His family has been growing plants for Kansas City ever since — through droughts, late frosts, and a whole lot of springs.

We're a Grow Native! member because we believe in planting things that belong here. Most of our stock is grown right on site. We know what survives a KC summer.
