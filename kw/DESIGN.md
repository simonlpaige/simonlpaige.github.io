# Kaleigh White contact card

## Direction

- Purpose: a fast, mobile-first business card at `simonlpaige.com/kw/`.
- Brand cues: Renewed Vision's near-black field, crisp white type, and measured orange accent (`#ff6c1b`).
- Content: only verified details supplied by Simon, including Kaleigh's attached portrait. Do not invent a job title, address, or social profiles.
- Actions: call, email, visit Renewed Vision, download vCard, share the page, and exchange contact information.
- Form: post only to the first-party Groundlayer worker, with Kaleigh's allowlisted recipient and a verified success redirect.
- Accessibility: semantic landmarks, visible focus states, 44px touch targets, reduced-motion support, and AA contrast.
- Forbidden: third-party contact forms, stock photography, generic SaaS gradients, and decorative motion.
