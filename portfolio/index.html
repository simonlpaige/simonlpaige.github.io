Platforms . & . Capabilities

What I work with every day.

DSP & Control

Q-SYS Level 2 certified. Extron Pro, Biamp, BSS, Crestron, AMX. I configure, tune, and troubleshoot DSP systems, and I work with control system programmers to identify and resolve snags during commissioning.

Unified Communications

I sell, install, and commission Microsoft Teams Rooms, Zoom Rooms, Google Meet, Webex, and Cisco-based environments. Full integration with audio, video, and control subsystems in multi-vendor deployments.

Professional Audio

L-Acoustics and d&b certified. Line arrays, distributed audio, assistive listening, live sound reinforcement for theaters, houses of worship, and performance venues.

Lighting & Stage Systems

ETC Lighting Certified Technician. ETC and Strand console programming, DMX distribution, architectural and theatrical lighting integration, and coordinated commissioning with audio and video subsystems.

AV-over-IP & Networking

TCP/IP, VLAN-aware planning, multicast concepts, device addressing, Dante-adjacent environments. I understand how AV rides on the network, and how to keep it from breaking the network.

Documentation & Handoff

As-built schematics, cable schedules, O&M documentation, and end-user training. My goal at handoff is that the client never has to call me to operate the room. But if something breaks, I answer.

Automation . & . Tools

I write tools to make AV work faster and more reliable.

Workflow Automation

Python scripts that automate device validation, check signal paths, and generate configuration documentation. What used to take an afternoon of manual testing now runs in 20 minutes, and catches errors I'd miss by hand.

Python Lua
AI & Autonomous Systems Research

Built multi-agent orchestration frameworks, RAG pipelines, and local inference infrastructure. Active research into how autonomous agents can improve AV operations, preventative maintenance, and system validation. Write in Python, Rust, and C++.

Python Rust C++
Commissioning Automation

Custom Lua scripts for Q-SYS and Extron control systems that automate room configuration, validate DSP settings, and run pre-commissioning checks before a client ever walks the room. Fewer punch-list items, faster sign-off.

Lua Q-SYS Extron
Open-Source AV Toolkit

A growing library of commissioning tools for Extron, Q-SYS, Biamp, Crestron, and general AV workflows — device scanners, config backups, signal path tracers, cable schedule generators, and uptime monitors. Built in Python, Lua, and C++. Available on GitHub.
