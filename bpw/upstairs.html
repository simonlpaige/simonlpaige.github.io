Climb the stairs. Find your character.

Most people who walk in for the first time come for balloons or paper goods. Then they hear about the upstairs. The attic is where the personality lives: rack after rack of costumes, walls of accessories, drawers of masks.

October is our biggest month, but we keep the attic stocked all year. Theme parties, 80s nights, school productions, costume contests, dance recitals, gender reveals, and the occasional cosplay run, we have helped with all of it.

If you are not sure what you need, climb the stairs and ask. We will help you put a look together.
