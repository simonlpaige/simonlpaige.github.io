A Brookside fixture, not a big-box stop.

For years, neighbors have walked into Brookside Party Warehouse on 63rd Street and walked out with the right balloons, the right invitations, and a few stories from owner Jerri. We are a one-stop party shop, but we are also a neighborhood store.

Two full floors of party goods downstairs. An attic full of costumes upstairs. And the largest selection of Caspari paper goods in Kansas City, hand-picked by us.

Personal service from people who know the difference between a 4th birthday and a 40th. Stop in, say hi, and let us help.
