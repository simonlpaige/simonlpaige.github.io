DESIGNED AROUND YOUR SPACE

Make the meeting room easy to use.

A productive meeting needs clear speech, readable content and a straightforward way to connect. Room size, seating, acoustics and the location of microphones, cameras and displays all affect the experience. We begin with the meetings you host and the problems your team encounters.

From our Lenexa office, AVDOXA works with Kansas City businesses and project teams to coordinate conferencing systems with the room infrastructure. Bring your preferred meeting platforms and IT requirements to the discussion so the equipment, network and user workflow can be evaluated together.

PLAN THE SCOPE

From the first conversation to the handoff.

Audio and camera coverage

Review seating, microphone pickup, loudspeaker coverage, acoustics and camera views so the design supports both local and remote participants.

Displays and collaboration

Plan displays, content sharing and connections around presentations, hybrid meetings and the platforms your organization uses. Confirm compatibility during design.

Controls and handoff

Develop a repeatable meeting workflow with clear controls. Coordinate network requirements, system commissioning, staff training and operating documentation.

BEFORE YOU START

Questions to bring to the table.

Can we improve our current conference room?

Yes. Share the room layout, existing equipment and the problems users report. We can discuss a focused upgrade or a broader plan after reviewing compatibility and room conditions.

Can the room support our preferred meeting platform?

Tell us which platforms and devices your team uses, including guest meeting needs and IT policies. We evaluate those requirements during design before defining the equipment and integration scope.
