DESIGNED AROUND YOUR SPACE

A worship system your team can operate.

A church sound system has to support more than a Sunday mix. Spoken teaching, live music, recorded content and an online audience may share the same room and production team. A new sanctuary, an aging installation or a growing ministry each calls for a different starting point. We begin with the room, the people using it and the experience you want to create.

From our Lenexa, Kansas office, AVDOXA works with ministry leaders, facility teams and construction partners. Church AV planning should connect loudspeaker coverage, video sightlines, lighting positions, network infrastructure and operator workflow. Treating these as one system helps the design serve both the congregation and the people behind the console.

PLAN THE SCOPE

From the first conversation to the handoff.

Audio and intelligibility

Plan sound reinforcement around speech, music, seating coverage and the room’s acoustic conditions. Review microphones, mixing, loudspeakers and the equipment worth retaining.

Video and production

Coordinate displays or LED video walls, cameras, recording and streaming with the sanctuary’s sightlines and content needs. Include network and signal paths in the plan.

Lighting and operation

Develop lighting and controls around the stage, presenters and camera needs. Define commissioning, operator training and documentation alongside the installation scope.

BEFORE YOU START

Questions to bring to the table.

Can we improve an existing church sound system?

Yes. A consultation can start with your current equipment, room layout and recurring problems. We can discuss a focused upgrade or a phased plan before deciding what needs replacement.

What should we bring to a consultation?

Bring room drawings or photos, seating layout, typical worship activities, streaming requirements and a list of the challenges your team experiences. Share your construction schedule and budget goals if available.
