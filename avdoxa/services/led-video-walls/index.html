DESIGNED AROUND YOUR SPACE

Choose the wall for the room and the content.

A successful LED video wall installation begins with what people need to see and where they will sit. Screen size alone does not determine the result. Viewing distance, image detail, ambient light, camera use and the content format all influence a display’s suitability for the space.

AVDOXA connects the display with the production system around it. We discuss how content reaches the wall, who operates it, how it works with other screens and cameras, and how it will be serviced. For church LED video walls, that includes lyrics, teaching visuals, live camera images and the weekly workflow of the ministry team.

PLAN THE SCOPE

From the first conversation to the handoff.

Display and sightline planning

Review screen dimensions, viewing distances, pixel pitch, brightness and content needs. Consider the nearest seats as well as the furthest audience areas.

Video system integration

Coordinate processing, signal routing, switching and operator controls. Discuss camera compatibility, content resolution and how the wall connects to streaming or other venue displays.

Infrastructure and maintenance

Define power, mounting coordination, service access and equipment locations with the project team. Include setup, commissioning and operator training in the installation plan.

BEFORE YOU START

Questions to bring to the table.

How do we choose a size and pixel pitch?

Start with audience distances, available wall area and the content you intend to show. Those details help determine the appropriate display dimensions and image detail rather than choosing from a specification alone.

Can an LED wall work with our existing church video system?

That can be evaluated during the consultation. We review current sources, switchers, signal formats, operator controls and streaming needs before defining the integration scope.
