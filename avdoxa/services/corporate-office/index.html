DESIGNED AROUND YOUR SPACE

A connected workplace, room by room.

An office brings together many types of spaces: conference rooms, training areas, reception, cafes and open collaboration zones. Each serves a different purpose, but staff should be able to move between them with confidence. We discuss how each space is used before developing the AV plan.

AVDOXA coordinates workplace technology with owners, IT teams, architects and construction partners. A renovation, expansion or new office is an opportunity to align equipment locations, cabling, network infrastructure and controls with the way your organization works.

PLAN THE SCOPE

From the first conversation to the handoff.

Meeting and training spaces

Plan audio, displays, content sharing and controls around room size, audience needs and staff workflows. Coordinate conferencing requirements where remote participation is needed.

Workplace audio and displays

Discuss distributed audio, presentation displays and signage for reception, cafes and shared spaces. Establish coverage, content sources and day-to-day operation.

Infrastructure and long-term operation

Coordinate equipment locations, cabling, networking and construction requirements. Include commissioning, documentation, staff training and ongoing service planning in the discussion.

BEFORE YOU START

Questions to bring to the table.

Can an office upgrade be phased?

We can discuss priorities by room, existing equipment and the renovation schedule. Compatibility, infrastructure and operational needs help determine a practical sequence.

When should AV planning begin?

Bring AVDOXA into the conversation while room layouts and infrastructure are being planned. Early coordination helps identify display locations, cabling pathways, power and network requirements before construction progresses.
