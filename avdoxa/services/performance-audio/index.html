DESIGNED AROUND YOUR SPACE

Start with the audience and the room.

An auditorium may host a speaker one day, a band the next and a community event that evening. Each use puts different demands on sound coverage, microphones, mixing and system control. Uneven levels, unclear speech or complicated setup can interrupt the experience even when individual components still work.

AVDOXA approaches performance audio installation as a coordinated system. We review the seating areas, stage uses, room conditions and production workflow before selecting equipment. Based in Lenexa, Kansas, our team works with owners and design partners on renovations and new spaces, with video and lighting coordination when the event requires it.

PLAN THE SCOPE

From the first conversation to the handoff.

Coverage and room conditions

Review audience areas, speaker positions and acoustic conditions. Establish priorities for spoken-word clarity, musical performance and consistent listening across the venue.

Production and connections

Plan microphones, console functions, signal routing, amplification and networking around the events you host. Consider guest operators, visiting productions and useful existing equipment.

Commissioning and handoff

Include system setup, control programming, commissioning and operator training in the project discussion. Define repeatable workflows for staff who prepare the room between events.

BEFORE YOU START

Questions to bring to the table.

Can one auditorium system support several event types?

That is a useful design goal. We discuss the event calendar, required inputs, audience areas and operator experience, then plan the equipment and control workflow around those uses.

Can audio be upgraded while retaining video and lighting?

Often, a phased approach is worth evaluating. Existing connections, control compatibility and the condition of retained equipment need review before the installation scope is set.
