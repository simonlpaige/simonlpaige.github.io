How AVDOXA handles website inquiries and the services you choose to use.

Updated October 10, 2026

About this notice

AVDOXA is the trade name of SRS Commercial AVL LLC. This notice covers this website and the AVDOXA inquiry forms linked from it. It does not describe separate employment records or the operation of systems installed at client locations.

Information you provide

When you contact us, we receive the information you enter, such as your name, email address, phone number, organization, project or equipment details, and message. A project inquiry may also include how you heard about us. Please do not include passwords, payment-card details, Social Security numbers or other sensitive information in a website form.

How inquiries are handled

We use your information to respond to your request, discuss a project, arrange service and keep records of our communications. Our forms are processed through AVDOXA’s WordPress website. Submissions are saved there and emailed to the AVDOXA staff or team mailbox responsible for your request.

Website hosting, form-processing and email providers process information as part of delivering those services. Technical requests to our website and its providers may include your IP address, browser information, requested page and request time. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

Browser preferences and third-party services

The website follows your device’s light or dark theme. If you choose a different theme, that choice is stored in your browser’s session storage.

Video preview images are requested from YouTube’s image servers. The embedded YouTube player loads when you select a video. YouTube may receive technical information when its images or player load and may use cookies or similar technologies under its own policies. Event registration opens Ticket Tailor. Registration information is processed for the event organizer, which is responsible for explaining its collection and use; review the notice and terms presented during registration. Maps, social media and other external links open services with their own privacy practices.

You can control cookies and browser storage through your browser settings. The website’s front end does not change its behavior in response to a browser Do Not Track signal. Third-party services may respond differently. For more information, review Google’s privacy policy and Ticket Tailor’s provider privacy information.

Questions and requests about your information

Contact info@avdoxa.com or 913.383.0243 to ask about information you submitted, request a correction or request deletion. We review requests under applicable law and may need to verify your identity. Business or legal recordkeeping requirements may affect what can be deleted. No website or email transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You can also write to AVDOXA, 10413 W. 84th Terrace, Lenexa, KS 66214. Changes to this notice will appear on this page with an updated date.

Website information and service agreements

Project photos and case studies illustrate particular installations. A suitable system, project scope and results depend on your space and requirements. Website descriptions do not replace a project proposal, warranty or service agreement. Pricing, coverage, response commitments and exclusions are set out in the applicable agreement.

Submitting a form does not confirm a service appointment or project engagement. For time-sensitive service needs, call 913.383.0243. After-hours availability and response times are not guaranteed.

Help using the website

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, contact help@avdoxa.com or 913.383.0243. Tell us which page or feature you were using and how we can help. See our accessibility information.
