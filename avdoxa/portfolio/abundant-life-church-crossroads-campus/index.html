House of Worship · Kansas City, Missouri

Abundant Life, Crossroads

A downtown campus transformation with modern production, networking, and gathering-space technology.

THE PROJECT

Designed for the experience.

Kansas City, Missouri. AVDOXA partnered with Abundant Life as they renovated an existing building to launch their brand-new Crossroads Campus in downtown Kansas City. The transformed space features upgraded audio, video, and networking systems throughout, along with an inviting outdoor courtyard designed for connection and community. This project brings modern ministry capability into the heart of one of Kansas City's most vibrant districts.
