House of Worship · Blue Springs, Missouri

Blue Springs Christian Church

A connected central lobby with clear distributed audio, dynamic signage, and a resilient network backbone.

THE PROJECT

Designed for the experience.

AVDOXA partnered with Blue Springs Christian Church in Blue Springs, Missouri on a new-build project designed to connect the church's two existing buildings with a brand-new central lobby. This addition created a unified, welcoming space for guests and members, while giving the church an opportunity to upgrade and expand its audio, video, and networking infrastructure.

Working closely with Pearce Construction and Mantel Teter, our team designed and integrated a modern AVL system tailored for the new lobby and adjoining spaces. The installation features QSC loudspeakers for clear, consistent audio throughout the environment, paired with Samsung displays to support digital signage, announcements, and dynamic visual content. To ensure reliability behind the scenes, we implemented robust NETGEAR networking solutions, providing the backbone needed for seamless system performance.

The completed project not only enhances communication and connection within the church but also creates a future-ready foundation that supports ministry growth and community engagement.
