House of Worship · Omaha, Nebraska

Lord of Hosts Church

A high-capacity Tabernacle and studio built for immersive worship, broadcast, and content creation.

THE PROJECT

Designed for the experience.

Omaha, Nebraska. AVDOXA partnered with Lord of Hosts Church on the renovation and construction of their new Tabernacle, a modern auditorium designed to support immersive worship and high-capacity production. As long-time partners, we were honored to help bring their vision to life through a full audio, video, and lighting integration.

The project features technology from trusted brands such as Panasonic, ETC, Blackmagic, Absen, Chamsys, QSC, and more, delivering powerful performance, exceptional clarity, and long-term reliability. In addition to the Tabernacle upgrades, AVDOXA also provided camera and lighting equipment for their studio, supporting their expanding broadcast and content creation needs.
