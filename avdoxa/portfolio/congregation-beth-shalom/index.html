House of Worship · Overland Park, Kansas

Congregation Beth Shalom

A sanctuary modernization with immersive sound, streamlined production, and dependable streaming infrastructure.

THE PROJECT

Designed for the experience.

Overland Park, Kansas. AVDOXA partnered with Congregation Beth Shalom to upgrade their sanctuary with a modern audio and video system. The installation included QSC speakers for clear, immersive sound, BirdDog video solutions for seamless streaming and recording, and NETGEAR networking infrastructure to support reliable performance. The upgraded sanctuary now offers enhanced worship experiences and streamlined production capabilities.
