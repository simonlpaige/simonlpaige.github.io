An auditorium renovation pairing OHR LED visuals, NEXO sound, and a reliable foundation for long-term ministry.

Working alongside Pearce Construction and Mantel Teter, AVDOXA partnered with Emmanuel Baptist Church to renovate and modernize their existing auditorium through a comprehensive AVL systems integration approach. The goal was to enhance the worship experience by upgrading the room's audio and video technology while creating a reliable, scalable infrastructure to support the church's long-term ministry needs.

As part of the renovation, AVDOXA installed OHR LED screens, delivering high-resolution visuals, improved brightness, and flexible content capabilities to support worship services, teaching, and special events. To complement the visual upgrades, the auditorium's sound system was enhanced with NEXO loudspeakers, providing clear, intelligible, and evenly distributed audio throughout the space, ensuring every message and musical moment is heard with clarity.

By combining thoughtful audio system design, video integration, and professional AVL engineering, the finished auditorium creates a more immersive and engaging worship environment. The updated technology not only elevates production quality but also provides a future-ready foundation that allows Emmanuel Baptist Church to grow, adapt, and serve its congregation with confidence for years to come.
