A complete AVL environment for a new worship space, designed for engaging in-person and online experiences.

As part of Fellowship Church's facility expansion, our team provided a complete audio, video, and lighting solution for the new worship space. From system design and engineering to installation, programming, and commissioning, the project delivered a fully integrated AVL environment designed to support engaging worship experiences for both in-person and online audiences. Key components included a QSC loudspeaker system, a large-format OHR LED video wall, professional stage lighting, and user-friendly control systems.
