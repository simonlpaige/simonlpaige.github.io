A versatile event center upgraded for weddings, corporate events, and special occasions from 350 to 1,000 guests.

Unity Village, Missouri. AVDOXA partnered with The R.D. Goppert Event Center at Unity Village to deliver a full audio, video, and lighting upgrade for this versatile, newly renovated venue. Designed to accommodate 350 to 1,000 guests, the event center features a flexible main floor, an accessible balcony, and modern amenities that ensure a seamless experience for weddings, corporate events, and special occasions.

Our team integrated state-of-the-art AV systems including Allen & Heath consoles, Blackmagic video solutions, Chauvet lighting, EAW and QSC audio, Shure microphones, and RESI automation systems to create an immersive, reliable, and highly adaptable environment. The project enhances the main event spaces, lobby for receptions, and picturesque grounds, providing stunning audio, video, and lighting experiences for every event.

The upgraded event center now offers a balanced blend of elegance and technology, helping every gathering at Unity Village feel considered and well supported.
