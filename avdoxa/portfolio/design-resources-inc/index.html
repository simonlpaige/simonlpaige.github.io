Integrated technology that supports themed conference rooms, cafes, open meeting areas, and a creative workplace story.

Overland Park, Kansas. AVDOXA partnered with Design Resources Inc. to support their expanding office with an integrated audio and video upgrade that complements the creative and collaborative design of the space. The evolving office features themed conference rooms, cafes, and open meeting areas, all crafted to foster communication and establish a cohesive narrative throughout the workspace.

Our installation includes QSC speakers for high-quality, immersive audio and Samsung displays to support digital collaboration, presentations, and real-time communication. The technology seamlessly integrates with the office's flexible design, enhancing both functionality and the overall employee experience.

The result is a dynamic, connected office environment where creativity, collaboration, and storytelling thrive through thoughtful AV integration.
