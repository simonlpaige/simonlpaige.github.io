Why these components

A complete rig, not a loose equipment list.

Canon EOS C400 6K Camera

A next-generation cinema camera with a 6K full-frame sensor, advanced autofocus, internal RAW recording, and an ergonomic form factor that adapts to a range of production environments.

Canon CINE-SERVO 25-250mm T2.95 Lens

Wide-to-telephoto coverage in a single lens, with a fast T2.95 aperture and a servo kit for precise zoom control.

Canon EF-EOS R Adapter

A direct bridge between Canon RF and EF lens ecosystems for broader lens compatibility.

Kondor Blue support system

An adjustable baseplate, 15mm rods, universal lens support, and C400 cage create a stable, configurable production rig.

Cartoni Focus 10

Professional tripod support and a second telescopic pan bar provide smooth control for heavier cinema and broadcast builds.

Production-ready media

High-capacity SanDisk media and a rugged Pelican case support long 4K and 6K shoots.
