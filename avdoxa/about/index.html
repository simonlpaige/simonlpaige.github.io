OUR STORY

Built on experience.
Guided by service.

AVDOXA has designed and integrated commercial audio and visual systems across Kansas City and nationwide since 2001, when Dan and Jess Stark founded the company as Stark Raving Solutions.

Retired naval veteran Greg Davis purchased the company in 2020, bringing a passion for AV and the principles of safety, quality and discipline he learned in naval aviation. He renamed it AVDOXA in 2022, the same year it was certified as a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB).

That commitment to service shapes our work with houses of worship, performance venues, businesses and public agencies. We bring consultation, engineering, installation, commissioning, training and long-term support together so owners can focus on what happens in the room.
