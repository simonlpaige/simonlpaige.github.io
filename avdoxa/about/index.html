Our story & what we do

A company built around the experience.

AVDOXA brings together consultation, engineering, craftsmanship, training, and long-term support so people can communicate clearly and confidently in the rooms that matter to them.

Based in Kansas City since 2001

An AVL integrator you can trust.

AVDOXA designs audio, video, lighting, acoustic, broadcast, and control systems for spaces of all sizes, from large church auditoriums and performance venues to public facilities and business conference rooms.

Our work can be found across North America, including Alaska, and in several international locations. With every project, we focus on client-centered solutions that elevate the way people experience media and communication.

What sets AVDOXA apart is our commitment to exceptional consultation, quality craftsmanship, and outstanding customer service. We stay responsive and adaptable, providing thoughtful design, precise installation, and ongoing support long after project completion.

What we do

Help people share their message in the most powerful way possible.

We design and install systems that do more than work. They support connection, clarity, collaboration, worship, performance, learning, and public service.

Listen first

Understand the goals, people, workflows, constraints, budget, and future plans before selecting technology.

Engineer the whole room

Coordinate audio, video, lighting, acoustics, networks, infrastructure, control, accessibility, and operations as one experience.

Build with accountability

Install, configure, test, commission, document, and hand off systems with precision and clear ownership.

Stay connected

Train the people who use the system, respond when help is needed, and support the relationship beyond installation.
