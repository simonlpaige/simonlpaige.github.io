OUR STORY

Built on experience.
Guided by service.

  1. 2001Dan and Jess Stark launch Stark Raving Solutions.
  2. 2020Greg Davis purchases the company.
  3. 2022AVDOXA rebrand and SDVOSB certification.

AVDOXA has designed and integrated high-end commercial audio and visual systems across Kansas City and nationwide since 2001, when Dan and Jess Stark founded the company as Stark Raving Solutions.

In 2020, retired naval veteran Greg Davis purchased the company, bringing a passion for AV and the principles of safety, quality and discipline he learned in naval aviation. He renamed the company AVDOXA in 2022, the same year it was certified by the Veterans Administration as a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB).

Public service runs through Greg’s family: his son served in the United States Marine Corps, and his brother dedicated his career to law enforcement in the greater Seattle area. That commitment shapes AVDOXA’s mission to deliver exceptional commercial AV systems and uncompromising service for businesses, communities and government agencies, with a special dedication to law enforcement and military organizations.
