Tech Director Experience

A day created for the people behind the experience.

TDX brings church technical directors, production leaders, worship team members, and volunteers together for practical learning, honest conversation, and renewed purpose.

Register for TDX View the schedule

Connection, encouragement, and practical growth

Learn with people who understand the work.

You are invited to TDX, a gathering at Blue Springs Christian Church in Blue Springs, Missouri, with support from industry partners including Shure and Yamaha.

Whether you mix front of house, lead a team, support broadcast, or are just beginning in church production, the experience is designed to meet you where you are and help you grow with confidence.

  • Breakout sessions led by experienced voices
  • Time with industry partners
  • Hands-on training for church production teams
  • Roundtable conversations and relationship building

No one is meant to do this work alone. Come ready to learn, ask questions, and be part of the conversation.
